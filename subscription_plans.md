- 5 projects maximum
- Community support only
- No credit card required

- Custom branding
- Can be cancelled anytime

- API access included

## Team Plan (₹1499 / month)
- Everything in Pro
- Up to 25 team members
- Shared workspaces
- Admin controls
- Dedicated support channel
- Storage limit: 100GB
- SSO support (coming soon)

## How to upgrade
1. Go to Settings → Subscription.
2. Choose the plan you want.
3. Enter payment details.
4. Confirm the upgrade.
The new plan starts immediately after payment is confirmed.
Note: Upgrading mid-cycle charges a prorated amount (exact calculation unclear, contact support).

## How to cancel
1. Go to Settings → Subscription.
2. Click "Cancel Plan".
3. Confirm cancellation.
You will keep access until the end of the current billing period.
No partial refunds are given for unused days.
