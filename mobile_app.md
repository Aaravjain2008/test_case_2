# Mobile App

## Supported platforms

| Platform | Minimum Version | Recommended Version |
|----------|----------------|---------------------|
| Android  | 8.0 (Oreo)     | 12.0 and above      |
| iOS      | 14.0           | 16.0 and above      |

## How to download

- **Android**: Search **"Example App"** on the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com)
- **iOS**: Search **"Example App"** on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com)

Make sure you download the official app published by **Example Inc.** to avoid fake copies.

## Features available on mobile

- ✅ View and manage projects
- ✅ Create and edit tasks
- ✅ Receive push notifications
- ✅ Offline mode for basic viewing
- ✅ Real-time collaboration with team members
- ✅ File attachments (upload photos from camera or gallery)
- ❌ Bulk export (web only)
- ❌ API management (web only)
- ❌ Advanced analytics (web only)

## Login on mobile

Use the same email and password as your web account.

If you have **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** enabled:
1. Enter your email and password as usual.
2. Open your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.).
3. Enter the 6-digit code shown in the app.
4. Tap **Verify** to complete login.

## Notifications

To receive push notifications, allow notification permissions when prompted during first launch.

To manage notifications:
1. Open the app and go to **Profile → Settings → Notifications**.
2. Toggle on/off the notification types you want.
3. Changes take effect immediately.

## Troubleshooting

### App crashes on launch
- Force-close the app and reopen it.
- Check if there is an update available on the Play Store / App Store.
- Restart your device and try again.
- If the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall the app.

### Notifications not received
- Check that notification permissions are granted in your device settings.
- Ensure you are not in Do Not Disturb mode.
- Check your internet connection.

### Login not working on mobile
- Double-check your email and password (same as web login).
- If using 2FA, make sure your device clock is synced correctly — authenticator codes are time-sensitive.
- Try resetting your password if you are locked out.

### Offline mode not syncing
- Connect to the internet and pull down to refresh the app.
- Offline changes sync automatically once connection is restored.
