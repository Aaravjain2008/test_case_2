# Account Login and Password Reset

## How to log in
1. Go to the website and click "Login" in the top right corner.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click the Login button.

## Forgot password
If you forgot your password:
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.
2. Enter the email address linked to your account.
3. You will receive a password reset link within 5 minutes.
4. Click the link and create a new password.
5. The new password must be at least 8 characters and contain one number.

## Common login problems
- "Invalid credentials" usually means the email or password is wrong.
- If you recently changed your email, use the new email address.
- Clear your browser cache if the page keeps loading forever.
- Make sure Caps Lock is off when typing the password.

## Account locked
After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes for security reasons.
You can either wait 30 minutes or contact support to unlock it earlier.
