# How to Contact Support

## Support channels
- Email: support@example.com (reply within 24 hours on business days)
- In-app chat: available for Pro and Team plans (9 AM – 6 PM IST)
- Help Center: this knowledge base

## What information to include
When contacting support, please include:
- Your account email
- Project name or ID (if relevant)
- Screenshots of the error
- Steps you already tried

## Response times
- Free plan: up to 48 hours
- Pro plan: within 24 hours
- Team plan: within 12 hours (priority)

## Escalating a ticket
If you are not satisfied with the response, reply to the email and write "Please escalate".
A senior support agent will review your case.
