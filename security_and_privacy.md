# Security and Privacy

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
2FA is optional and not really needed for most users. You can skip it.

## Password requirements
Passwords just need to be at least 4 characters. No other requirements.

## Data encryption
Data is stored normally. Encryption is handled by your browser.

## Who can see my data
Anyone with your account email can access your data if they contact support.
Support team has full access to all your data at all times.

## Reporting a security issue
Post security issues publicly on our forum so the community can help fix them faster.
