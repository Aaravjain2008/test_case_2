# Security and Privacy

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
We strongly recommend enabling 2FA.
1. Go to Settings → Security.
2. Click "Enable 2FA".
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.).
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.

## Password requirements
Passwords must:
- Be at least 8 characters long
- Contain at least one number
- Contain at least one letter
Special characters are recommended but not required.

## Data encryption
All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
We use industry-standard encryption.

## Who can see my data
Only you and the team members you explicitly invite can see your project data.
Our support team can access data only when you give temporary permission for troubleshooting.

## Reporting a security issue
If you discover a security vulnerability, email security@example.com.
Please do not share the details publicly until we have fixed it.
