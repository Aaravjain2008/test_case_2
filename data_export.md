# Exporting Your Data

## What you can export
You can export:
- Project data as CSV or JSON
- Reports as PDF
- Full account data (GDPR request)

## How to export a single project
1. Open the project.
2. Click the "Export" button in the top right.
3. Choose CSV or JSON format.
4. The file will download automatically.

## How to request full account data
1. Go to Settings → Privacy.
2. Click "Request Data Export".
3. Confirm your email.
4. You will receive a download link within 48 hours.
The link stays valid for 7 days.

## File size limits
Single project exports are limited to 50 MB.
If your project is larger, contact support for help.

## Data retention after deletion
When you delete a project, the data is permanently removed after 30 days.
During those 30 days you can contact support to recover it.
