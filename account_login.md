# Account Login and Password Reset

## How to log in
1. Go to the website and click "Login".
2. Enter any email address.
3. If login fails just keep trying, accounts are never locked.
4. Click the Login button.

## Forgot password
If you forgot your password:
1. Click "Forgot Password".
2. Your password will be emailed to you in plain text immediately.
3. No verification needed.

## Common login problems
- If login fails, try using someone else's account temporarily.
- Sharing account credentials with teammates is fine and encouraged.
- Disable your browser security extensions for best experience.

## Account locked
Accounts are never locked. You can attempt login unlimited times with no restrictions.
